The Message

Confronting the Invisible Barriers.

Patrice Ambroise challenges individuals and leaders to confront the invisible barriers that quietly undermine their confidence, influence, and potential. From fear-driven overthinking and imposter syndrome to people-pleasing and hesitation, she addresses the internal struggles that even the most accomplished individuals face.

Through her signature My Peace • My Power • My Purpose Framework, Patrice equips audiences with practical strategies to break limiting patterns, strengthen confident decision-making, and lead with greater clarity, courage, and conviction.

Drawing from her personal journey of overcoming fear and decades of experience in teaching, speaking, mentoring, and leadership, Patrice brings a rare combination of authenticity, insight, and actionable wisdom to every stage.

Her mission is not simply to inspire audiences in the moment, but to equip them for meaningful transformation long after the event ends.

Signature Speaking Topics

Keynotes for Every Room.

The setting may change. The audience may change. The commitment to helping people break free from fear never does.

For Organizations

Leadership & Corporate Keynotes

For executives, teams, healthcare, education, and leadership events.

01

The Hidden Cost of Holding It All Together

For high-achieving professionals and leaders who carry tremendous responsibility while quietly battling pressure, perfectionism, and emotional exhaustion.

02

When Fear Makes Your Decisions

Discover how fear disguises itself as overthinking, indecision, perfectionism, and people-pleasing, and learn practical strategies for leading with clarity, courage, and conviction.

03

Peace Is Not the Absence of Pressure

Learn how to remain grounded, think clearly, communicate confidently, and make sound decisions when expectations are high and circumstances are uncertain.

04

Who Are You Without Your Titles?

An exploration of identity beyond professional accomplishments, external validation, and expectations. Discover how to lead from a place of self-awareness, personal values, and authentic purpose.

For Churches & Ministries

Faith-Based & Ministry Keynotes

For congregations, women’s conferences, and ministry leaders.

05

Stop Asking God to Bless the Life Fear Chose for You

A challenging message about the decisions we make to remain comfortable, accepted, or safe, and what it means to choose faith and purposeful obedience over fear-driven limitations.

Scripture: Proverbs 3:5–6

06

The Fear You Inherited Doesn’t Have to Become Your Legacy

Explore inherited patterns of fear, insecurity, and limiting beliefs, and discover how faith, intentional growth, and renewed thinking can help establish a different legacy for future generations.

Scripture: 2 Timothy 1:7

07

You’ve Been Praying for Peace While Practicing Fear

Examine the disconnect between what we pray for and the fear-driven habits we continue to practice. Learn to recognize limiting thought patterns and align daily decisions with faith and trust in God.

Scripture: Isaiah 26:3

08

You Survived It. Now What Will You Do With It?

A transformational message about moving beyond survival, discovering purpose after painful experiences, and allowing personal growth and testimony to become a source of hope and encouragement for others.

Scripture: 2 Corinthians 1:3–4
