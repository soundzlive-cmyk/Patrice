Her Story

Beyond the Titles, Just Call Me Patrice.

Her Excellency Ambassador Patrice Ambroise, CGPA, carries many titles, but there’s one name she prefers you remember: Patrice.

  • Wife
  • Mother
  • Grandmother
  • Pastor
  • Ambassador
  • Entrepreneur
  • Author
  • Empowerment Strategist
  • Former New England Patriots Cheerleader

Each title represents a different chapter of her journey, but none tells her entire story.

Behind the accomplishments is a woman who understands what it means to face fear, navigate uncertainty, overcome self-doubt, and discover the courage to become who she was created to be.

As the owner of Spirit of Excellence Dance Studio, a pastor, speaker, and life coach, Patrice has spent years empowering others to recognize their potential and move beyond the limitations holding them back.

Her personal experiences, leadership journey, and faith became the foundation for her signature My Peace • My Power • My Purpose Framework, a transformational approach designed to help individuals reclaim their peace, rediscover their power, and live intentionally in their purpose.

Because for Patrice, true success has never been about the titles you carry. It’s about the person you become and the lives you impact along the way.

What She Does

Peace Is the Foundation From Which True Power Is Built.

Patrice Ambroise is a transformational speaker, leadership strategist, and empowerment catalyst who helps people navigate seasons of adversity, rediscover their identity, and develop the mindset and tools necessary to create meaningful change.

Through powerful storytelling, practical strategy, and authentic connection, Patrice teaches audiences that peace is not the absence of challenges — it is the foundation from which true power is built.

In Her Words

Performing Under Pressure. Conquering Fear.

I bring a unique combination of leadership, lived experience, faith, teaching, coaching, and transformational speaking to my work. As an empowerment speaker, author, entrepreneur, Peace Ambassador to the U.N., former New England Patriots Cheerleader, and organizational leader, I understand what it means to perform under pressure while conquering fear, self-doubt, and uncertainty.

My work focuses on helping individuals and leaders identify the fear-driven patterns that can lead to overthinking, imposter syndrome, people-pleasing, hesitation, and diminished confidence. Through my Peace • Power • Purpose framework, I help people move beyond those patterns, strengthen their confidence, make courageous decisions, and lead with greater clarity and purpose.

My personal journey of breaking free from fear, combined with years of teaching, speaking, mentoring, and leading others, allows me to connect personal transformation with practical leadership development.

My Mission

Peace is not weakness. Peace is power.

  1. When you find peace, you unlock your confidence.
  2. When you unlock your confidence, you discover your purpose.
  3. And when you begin living in your purpose, your life becomes the permission someone else needs to transform theirs.

Because the most powerful person in any room isn’t the loudest.
It’s the one who has found peace within.
