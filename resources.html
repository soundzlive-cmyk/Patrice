A Note From Patrice

I Didn’t Just Write About Fear. I Had to Break Free From It.

There was a time when fear influenced more of my life than I wanted to admit.

It affected my confidence, shaped my decisions, and caused me to question the very abilities and opportunities God had placed before me.

But my story did not end there.

Through faith, personal reflection, and the courage to confront deeply rooted patterns, I began a journey that changed how I thought, how I led, and how I lived. I share that journey in my book, Addicted to Fear: My Journey to Freedom.

Today, whether I’m speaking to corporate executives, coaching individuals, or ministering in a church, I carry the same conviction: no one should have to surrender their peace, silence their voice, or abandon their purpose because of fear.

This guide brings together the foundational lessons that have shaped my transformation and the work I now share with others.

My faith is not something I leave behind when I enter a boardroom. It is the foundation that allows me to enter every room with authenticity, integrity, and purpose.

The setting may change. The audience may change. But my commitment to helping people break free from fear never does.
